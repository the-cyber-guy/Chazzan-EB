What I Do

I bring Carlebach-style acoustic davening, energy and musical ruach to Orthodox communities across America. My passion is creating moments where an entire kehillah sings together — naturally, powerfully and joyfully.

Whether it's a soulful Kabbalat Shabbat, uplifting Yom Tov davening, a wedding, Bar Mitzvah Shabbat, intimate kumzits or a Havdalah concert, the goal is always the same: authentic connection through music and tefillah.

All davening follows traditional halachic minhagim and is conducted entirely in Hebrew. I play guitar, piano and a wide range of instruments, and am an experienced vocalist, Ba'al Tefillah and Jewish entertainer available nationwide across the USA.

About Elliott

I'm a British-born Israeli — raised in and around community my whole life. I grew up playing guitar, piano, drums and a whole range of instruments from the age of five. Music, davening and communal singing have always been at the heart of who I am.

I've been privileged to lead davening and perform for hundreds of communities worldwide — from Ohel Leah Synagogue in Hong Kong to the Mazkeret Batya Great Synagogue in Israel, where I serve as Chazzan. I spent years as Choir Master at the Pretoria Hebrew Congregation in South Africa, and have been leading High Holiday davening for over 18 years.

Today I'm based in the USA on shlichut as a diplomatic spouse, and I'm also known in another world entirely as “The Cyber Guy” — an Israeli hi-tech representative. But when Shabbat comes in, all of that falls away. My heart is with my guitar, my voice and the kehillah in front of me.

I believe there is nothing more powerful than a room full of people singing together as one. That's what I live for. That's what I bring to every community I serve.
